⛵ Sail Builder

SAIL RACE
The job: sail out the inlet to the ocean faster than the serpent (6 knots), carrying yourself (60 kg) and 6 stones (5 kg each). The wind blows 20 knots from behind you.
Supplies: 70 sticks (1.13 m each, cut them shorter if you like) · Monster skin ×2 (3.05 × 2.73 m) · one cloth sail (2.16 × 2.79 m)
GULP! 🐉
Draw your boat's cross-section: the shape you'd see if you sawed it in half across the middle. Drag from one point to another to glue in a stick (or click one end, then the other).
Tool
Every stick can be cut shorter, never longer than 1.13 m, and has to be glued to another stick. The grid squares are 10 cm. You sit in the middle (the dashed line) and need 50 cm of room; the mast stands there too.

The monster skin wraps round the outside of your frame by itself: under the boat, up its sides, and over both ends. Each piece is cut from one 3.05 × 2.73 m sheet.
