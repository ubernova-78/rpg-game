Measure & Average
Measure items using calipers (mm), rulers (cm), or tape measures (m), then calculate the average of the set.
Read the calipers to the whole millimeter, the ruler to the tenth of a centimeter, and the tape to the hundredth of a meter. Round every average to the nearest hundredth.
If a measurement is wrong you'll be told which one, and you measure it again. After two more tries the answer is shown.

Three sets — millimeters, then centimeters, then meters — 12 measurements between them. Each set is worth 10 points per measurement in it, awarded only if every measurement and the average are right the first time. 120 points possible.
