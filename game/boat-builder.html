⛵ Boat Builder

LAKE QUEST
The job: carry 60 stones (5 kg each) and yourself (60 kg) to the island in one trip. Build a wooden frame, stretch the monster skin (one 2 m × 2 m sheet) over it to keep the water out, then test it: put yourself in first, then load the stones. Loose stones slide around when the boat moves. Dividers on the floor stop them.
